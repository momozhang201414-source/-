# Teens in Times (TNT) — 时代少年团

## Overview
**Teens in Times (TNT)** is a 7-member Chinese boy group formed by **Time Fengjun Entertainment**.  
The group officially debuted on **November 23, 2019**, and is known for its strong vocal line, synchronized choreography, and long-term growth-oriented development.

- **Chinese Name:** 时代少年团  
- **English Name:** Teens in Times (TNT)  
- **Debut Date:** 2019-11-23  
- **Company:** Time Fengjun Entertainment  
- **Members:** 7  
- **Genre:** Mandopop, Dance Pop  
- **Fanbase Name:** Popcorn (爆米花)  
- **Official Color:** Orange
